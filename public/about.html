About

An open-source environmental tech venture.

HAZE is an environmental technology venture focusing on tackling one of the largest issues facing India: pollution.

As pollution levels increased in India I began to notice how air quality was negatively affecting the population, and altering the landscape of our National Capital. The lack of data on exactly where pollution was coming from was also limiting how effectively policies were being made. HAZE was created to tackle this issue.

By using electronics and data we are creating new technology to help understand where pollution is originating from. We aim to understand how pollution disperses and identify what type of pollution we are dealing with.

Why in the open

While proprietary sensors are designed to make it impossible to interrogate the underlying assumptions, and therefore the data becomes invalid once challenged in a legal setting. Haze is completely transparent. By making our every step and faults public, we are able to demonstrate that affordable hardware can be used to create verifiable evidence that can withstand scrutiny from regulators.
