The Beginning

With the rise of air pollution in India I noticed the different ways in which pollution affected public health while smog altered the landscape of our national capital. I noticed the unavailability of accurate data pinpointing pollution sources affected efficient policy making. That is the problem I wanted to fix with HAZE.

The Problem

While working on this, I ran into a problem I hadn't anticipated. I realized that while particulate matter can be measured accurately, attributing it to a specific emission source in real time is a much harder and still a largely unsolved problem. Separating one construction site's contribution from traffic, other nearby sources, and background pollution turns out to be ongoing research, not something I could solve through sensor placement or guesswork using wind direction alone.

Lumped Abstractions

The lumped abstraction is the hall pass that lets me draw circuits in the first place. It says that I can treat wires as ideal and ignore electromagnetic fields as long as the circuit is small compared to the wavelength of the signals in it. 9600 baud UART is a wavelength of kilometers, so a 15 cm sensor cable is just a wire. For HAZE that holds comfortably.
0062 Aug 2026 · Learning outcome

UART/start bit

UART does not have a clock wire; both ends agree on the baud rate and use their own timers to sample the incoming data. This is how PMS5003 and ESP32 communicate at 9600 baud without a clock line, resetting the receiver timer on the falling edge of the start bit for every byte.
0073 Aug 2026 · Learning outcome

Working of a scattering PM sensor

The sensor shines a laser beam through an air sample and measures how much light is scattered by particles in it. The size of a particle defines how much light is scattered to determine the size distribution, which is used to derive mass concentration under assumptions of density and optical cross-section.

I designed my very first Prototype, V0. The Asymmetry of Blame

Looking at construction sites around Delhi NCR, it is clear that contractors are also suffering from this issue. During winter crop burning and weather inversions, ambient air exceeds 400–500 µg/m³. With only a single-point monitor, regional smog cannot be separated from site dust, leading to sudden stop-work orders and 24/7 anti-smog cannon diesel waste.
03329 Oct 2026 · Update

The Solution: Haze Grid Pro

Haze Grid Pro, a 3–4 node mesh system, uses solar-powered ESP32-S3 units with size-binning optics (PMS5003), triple gas sensors (MiCS-6814), microclimate sensors (BME280), and wind direction sensors to separate mineral dust from diesel fumes and regional smoke, triggering misting only when site dust crosses 75 µg/m³.
03429 Oct 2026 · Update

Haze Duo—for smaller sites

HAZE Duo uses two solar-powered cellular nodes installed at opposite boundaries of your site (upwind and downwind). It provides true net dust emissions from your site by continuously calculating the difference between background regional smog coming into the site and the air going out.
03529 Oct 2026 · Update

Haze Legal Sentinel—For High Risk Infrastructure

Haze Sentinel combines the multi-node Haze Grid Pro spatiotemporal mesh with the Haze Duo dual sensor network and an automated compliance report generator for high-risk mega-projects, producing timestamped Legal Defense Dossiers and logging automated misting actions.
