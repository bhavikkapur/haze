Unverifiable data is unacceptable.

Proprietary sensors are designed to make it impossible to interrogate their underlying assumptions, which renders their data invalid the moment it is challenged in a legal setting.

Haze is completely transparent. By making every step, calibration curve, and fault public, we demonstrate that affordable hardware can create verifiable evidence that withstands scrutiny from regulators.

Where the project stands today.

Last updated 18 October 2026

Prototype V0 & Calibration

With Prototype V0 active in the field logging baseline particulate matter readings, our immediate focus is sensor calibration—aligning low-cost optical hardware with reference-grade instruments so affordable nodes deliver reliable, repeatable accuracy under real ambient conditions.

Lab Outreach & ML Baselines

Our goal is to establish rigorous calibration baselines that produce measurements regulators and communities can trust. To get there, we are currently reaching out to research labs to secure access to reference-grade calibration equipment alongside lab-grade, source-labeled datasets for machine learning.

The Beginning

It was when I saw construction sites in my area with visible dust emissions and uncovered sites with no accountability that the idea clicked. I wanted the residents living nearby and the construction workers whose health was compromised to be able to take action. While air quality monitoring websites did exist they did not attribute pollution to specific sources such as construction sites and thus the residents had no real proof to hold the concerned authorities accountable.
